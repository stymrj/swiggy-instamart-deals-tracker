Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. It runs in your own browser tab. No login, no account, nothing sent anywhere else.

How does it know my store?

It picks up your store from the Instamart page you have open. Make sure your delivery address is set first.

Why is it slow sometimes?

Each aisle takes a second or two. Pick fewer aisles for quicker results.
